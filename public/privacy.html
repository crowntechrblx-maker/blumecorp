Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Westbridge OS ("we", "us", "our", the "Service") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information about you when you sign in with your Roblox account and use the Service. It is written to reflect the principles of the UK/EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applies to all users regardless of location.

1. Who we are

The data controller responsible for your personal data is Blume Corporations, a part of Polaris Studios (United Kingdom), contactable at admin@blumecorp.uk. If you have any questions about this policy or how your data is handled, please contact us using that address.

2. What data we collect

When you sign in and use Westbridge OS, we collect and process the following:

  • Roblox account data: your Roblox user ID, username, display name, and avatar/headshot image, obtained via Roblox's OAuth2 sign-in flow.
  • Content you submit: messages you send to other users, posts and images you upload, and any other text or files you voluntarily submit within the Service.
  • Usage and activity data: login timestamps, which features you use, and moderation-relevant activity (for example, if a message or post is reported or removed).
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, and similar technical information collected automatically by our hosting provider for security and reliability purposes.

We do not knowingly collect financial information, government ID numbers, or special category data (as defined by GDPR Article 9).

3. Why we process your data (legal basis)

  • Performance of a contract: to create your account, authenticate you via Roblox, and provide the core functionality of the Service (messaging, posting, and other in-app features).
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the Service secure, prevent abuse, moderate harmful or prohibited content (including profanity and slurs), and maintain an audit trail of platform activity for accountability.
  • Legal obligation: where we are required to retain or disclose information to comply with applicable law.
  • Consent: where you have voluntarily provided information (for example, uploading a background image), your continued use constitutes your consent to that specific processing, which you may withdraw at any time by deleting the content or contacting us.

4. How we use your data

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Service.
  • To display your username, avatar, and content to other users as intended by the Service's features.
  • To enforce our Terms of Service, including content moderation and, where necessary, suspending or banning accounts that violate our policies.
  • To maintain an internal audit log of account activity for security and accountability purposes, accessible only to platform administrators.

5. Who we share your data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share limited data with the following categories of third-party processors, solely to operate the Service:

  • Roblox Corporation — for authentication via Roblox OAuth2 and to retrieve your public profile information and group memberships.
  • Vercel Inc. — our hosting and infrastructure provider, which processes data on our behalf to serve the application and store uploaded files.
  • Upstash — our database provider, used to store account, message, and content data.

These providers are contractually bound to process data only on our instructions and in accordance with applicable data protection law.

6. International data transfers

Our infrastructure providers may process and store data in countries outside your own, including the United States. Where this occurs, we rely on appropriate safeguards recognized under GDPR, such as Standard Contractual Clauses, to ensure your data continues to receive an adequate level of protection.

7. Data retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account remains active. Messages and posts are retained until you delete them or your account is removed. Audit log entries are retained for a limited rolling period for security and accountability purposes. If you request deletion of your account, we will erase or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain certain records by law.

8. Your rights under GDPR

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten") of your personal data.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing of your data.
  • Data portability, receiving your data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at admin@blumecorp.uk. We will respond within one month, as required by GDPR.

9. Children's privacy

Because the Service is accessed via Roblox accounts, some users may be minors. We do not knowingly collect more data than necessary from any user, and we apply the same protections in this policy to all users regardless of age. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided us with personal data beyond what is necessary, please contact us so we can address it.

10. Cookies and similar technologies

We use a single, strictly necessary session cookie to keep you signed in. This cookie does not track you across other websites and is not used for advertising. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

11. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including signed, tamper-evident session tokens and access controls limiting administrative functions to authorized platform administrators. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

13. Contact us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us at admin@blumecorp.uk.
